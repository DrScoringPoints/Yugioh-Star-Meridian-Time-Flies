# Yu-Gi-Oh! Star Meridian: Time Flies

A fan-made, personal-use Yu-Gi-Oh! game for Windows and Android, built on the open-source EDOPro engine. It's inspired by Tag Force 3, with every anime era from Duel Monsters to VRAINS. Not affiliated with Konami, and not for sale.

## Current features
- **Story Mode**: student life at Meridian University. Pick a Tag Force-style partner from any era, explore a 3D overworld, take on duelists from every era, and work toward graduation.
- **Free Duel**: duel any of 250 era-accurate duelists with your own deck, a borrowed deck or a rental deck. Tag up with any partner from any era, including over 50 Original Characters.
- **Tournaments**: build a custom bracket with Singles (up to 32), Tag (up to 64) and CPU vs CPU. Watch or skip each match.
- **Star Circuit**: preset tournament cups to climb. The Field Cups ladder is coming (see Star Circuit under Planned).
- **Toon Corner Card Shop**: real booster sets priced by age, daily singles, the Creator's Private Collection (15,000 DP) and buy-once Starter and Structure Decks.
- **Trunk and Deck Editor**: collect cards, build 40-card decks and use duelist deck recipes. Follows the current TCG rules, with an optional Forbidden & Limited list.
- **Minigames**: DP-earning minigames such as Blackjack, darts, Card Toss Range, Attribute Sort, and Spirit Shell
- **Duel features**: a pause that truly freezes the duel, Restart Duel, and controller, keyboard, mouse and touch (Android) support.

### Star Circuit (Field Cups)
- **Star Circuit** is a tournament mode with 30 Field Cups. Each cup uses a real Field Spell as a house rule that applies to both players for the whole duel.
- The cup's field card sits in both Field Zones, can't be destroyed, and can't be bought or obtained anywhere. Neither player can play Field Spells.
- The menu is a cosmic orbit map: three rings of cups circling a sun.
- **Tier 1** is the outer ring: 10 cups with the simplest effects (like Umi or Yami), open from the start. Win **all** of them to light up Tier 2.
- **Tier 2** is the middle ring: 10 cups. Win all of them to unlock Tier 3.
- **Tier 3** is the inner ring: 9 cups with the most game-changing rules. Clear all of them to unlock the final cup on the sun: the **Seal of Orichalcos**.
- Earn a title for each step: Field Adept, Frontier Warden, Field Master and Field Sovereign. Prize DP goes up with each tier, and the first clear of a cup pays a bonus.
- Singles cups are 32-duelist brackets.
- Tag cups are 64 duelists in 32 teams and come in two modes, each with its own ladder:
  - **Knockout**: by default, nobody can attack until all 4 duelists have had a turn.
  - **Shared Field** (Tag Force style): each team shares one field and 8,000 LP, and attacks open on turn 2 like a normal duel.
- Singles, Tag Knockout and Tag Shared Field are separate ladders, and none of them unlocks another.
- Winning a cup unlocks its field rule as an option in Free Duel.
